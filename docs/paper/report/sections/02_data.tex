\paragraph{What is missing.} Hour is always recorded. The sensors and weather
go missing together, during whole-device outages on 3.9\% of hours; we drop
those 366 hours (8,991 remain), which leaves the sensors fully observed and the
inputs complete, since inputs have no CPT and cannot be inferred. The reference
gases are missing far more often (\cref{fig:missing}): CO on 18.0\% of hours and
NO$_x$ and NO$_2$ on 17.5\%. These gaps are concentrated at night, peaking at
54\% of CO readings at 4\,AM and 93\% of NO$_2$ readings at 3\,AM, consistent
with a scheduled analyzer calibration. Because the missingness depends on the
hour, which the model conditions on, rather than on the concentration itself,
we treat the gaps as missing at random \citep{dempster1977em}. The latent
regime $R$ has no column and is missing on every hour.
