\section{Dataset Source}
\label{sec:dataset}

We are using the UCI Air Quality dataset, which was collected by a roadside multisensor device in an Italian city from March 2004 to February 2005. 
The dataset contains 9,358 hourly averaged observations from five metal-oxide chemical sensors, reference-analyzer concentrations of carbon monoxide (CO), non-methanic hydrocarbons (NMHC), benzene, nitrogen oxides (NOx), and nitrogen dioxide (NO$_2$), as well as temperature and relative humidity.
Some known limitations include missing values encoded as $-200$, NMHC being mostly unusable because of missing data (90.2\% of hours, according to our analysis), and sensor drift and cross-sensitivity. 
During preprocessing, we plan to replace values encoded as $-200$ with a proper missing-value indicator, and continuous variables may be discretized into categories suitable for the probabilistic models.
We plan to use a time-based training and testing split to evaluate model performance on later observations, while accounting for missing measurements and gaps in the hourly sequence.
